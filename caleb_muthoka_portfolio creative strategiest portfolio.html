Strong creative work begins long before design, photography or video production starts. It begins with understanding the core problem, the audience, and the intended outcome.

05 // ABOUT

Curious by nature. Intentional by design.

I'm Caleb Muthoka, a Nairobi-based creative working across photography, graphic design, technology and digital content.

I'm interested in the space where a sharp idea meets thoughtful execution — where strategy gives creative work purpose and visual storytelling makes it memorable.

I'm building my practice around asking better questions, developing stronger concepts and helping creative ideas become clear, coherent experiences.

Leadership Philosophy

"Creative leadership is not about having every idea or controlling every detail. It's about establishing a clear direction, helping people understand the purpose of the work, and creating the conditions for good ideas to become excellent."
